Two lines sent back to a past self: a warning about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered.

## The entry

> Don't send the text you typed at 2 a.m. on the kitchen floor telling Maya 'I think we should stop seeing each other'.
>
> You will spend the next year learning the doubt was only exhaustion, and no apology ever unsends a sentence she believed.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly those two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (240 characters, inside the 1–280 gate).

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```
